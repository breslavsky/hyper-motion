# Folio 02 storyboard

Five authored phases share `compositions/document-flow.html`, six permanent
document objects and one camera. All timings are on the same 108 BPM grid;
there are no independent scene dispatches or hard cuts.

## Frame 1 — establish the protagonist
status: implemented
src: compositions/document-flow.html

Beats 0–5, 0–2.778s. “From scattered to sorted” leads; an enlarged, slightly
rotated Drafts sheet is the only visible document. Its unfinished abstract
artwork establishes a before-state. At 3.8–5 the sheet returns to its source
position while the camera's modest opening zoom returns to the board.
The document keeps its identity rather than being replaced by a hero duplicate.

## Frame 2 — collect the work
status: implemented
src: compositions/document-flow.html

## Frame 3 — arrange into categories
status: implemented
src: compositions/document-flow.html

Beats 10–22, 5.556–12.222s. “Arrange” accompanies one-at-a-time dealing.
The top corridor and bottom-up slot order keep carries away from settled
labels. Sheets turn face-up once clear of the queue. Folio's exact sequence is
Ideas → Sketches → Notes → Tasks → Drafts → Files, retaining original category
assignments. All three category names appear at beat 19.05, once the queue is
empty. The named board then holds for 1.639s. The delay is a known tradeoff:
early dealing shows the column structure before naming it.

## Frame 4 — complete the existing draft
status: implemented; native payoff frames viewed in all three formats
src: compositions/document-flow.html

Beats 22–26, 12.222–14.444s. “Create” and the same Drafts sheet become the
hierarchy. Other sheets dim first; the camera then approaches the sheet at its
real Files destination. At beats 23.4–25, its outlined photo fills, coral panel
expands and three abstract panels align. The completed artwork holds until
the end transition. This is a truthful visual metaphor with no added feature
claim. Native frames at beats 24.8 and 25.5 verify the late result in portrait,
square and wide. Long labels can suppress decorative art and weaken this beat.
